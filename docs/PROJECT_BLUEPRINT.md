# WheelPro Systems Project Blueprint

## Brand Summary

WheelPro Systems is a founder-led AI-powered systems agency built by Fahim Rahman. The brand exists to help mobile service businesses replace scattered calls, manual quote handling, slow follow-up, outdated websites, and disconnected tools with one connected digital operating system.

Core motto: "Turn Your Business Into a System"

Primary positioning: WheelPro Systems builds AI-powered websites, quote systems, booking flows, payment integrations, dashboards, B2B portals, and automation systems for mobile service businesses.

This is not positioned as a generic web design agency. The language should communicate a systems studio for quote-based, road-based, service businesses.

## Target Audience

Primary niche:

- Wheel refinishing shops
- Alloy and rim repair businesses
- Mobile wheel repair companies
- Mobile detailing businesses
- Fleet service providers
- Dealership service vendors
- Other service businesses operating on wheels

Primary decision maker:

- Owner-operators and small teams that rely on fast lead response, accurate quotes, bookings, payment collection, and follow-up.
- Businesses where missed calls, manual estimating, weak follow-up, and disconnected tools directly cost booked jobs.

## Website Goal

The website is a premium trust asset for cold outreach. It should make business owners feel that WheelPro Systems is not "a website guy" but a builder of practical business systems.

Primary conversion goal: Book a Free Growth Audit.

Secondary goal: Help visitors understand the system model before they book.

## Main Pages

- Home: Big idea, problem, system flow, offer, industries, founder-led trust, final CTA.
- Services: Service cards that connect business pain to system modules.
- Systems: Frontend demo-style product modules that show quote, booking, payment, dashboard, portal, and AI lead qualification workflows.
- Industries: Target verticals with wheel refinishing first.
- About: Founder-led positioning and honest trust language.
- Book: Free Growth Audit booking CTA with Google Calendar placeholder.
- Contact: Direct email contact and domain.

## Core Offer

AI Growth System for Mobile Service Businesses:

- Conversion-focused website
- Photo quote form
- Booking/calendar setup
- Deposit/payment integration
- Automated email/SMS follow-up
- Admin dashboard
- Monthly optimization/support

Pricing language: "Setup + monthly systems support"

No exact pricing is listed in the MVP.

## User Journey

1. Cold outreach visitor lands on the homepage.
2. Hero reframes the issue: most businesses have a system problem, not a lead problem.
3. Problem cards reflect their day-to-day friction.
4. System flow shows how lead, quote, booking, payment, and follow-up connect.
5. Services and systems pages prove the work is operational, not cosmetic.
6. Industries page confirms relevance for mobile and wheel-related workflows.
7. About page builds trust through founder-led clarity without fake claims.
8. Book page converts to a Free Growth Audit.

## Conversion Strategy

- Lead with a sharp, memorable promise: "Turn Your Business Into a System."
- Use repeated CTAs for "Book a Free Growth Audit."
- Make "Explore the System" the secondary path for visitors who need proof first.
- Replace abstract agency language with operational outcomes: faster lead response, cleaner quote intake, better booking, deposit collection, follow-up, dashboards, and portals.
- Avoid fake testimonials or inflated claims; build confidence through specificity and credible workflow design.

## Trust Strategy Without Fake Claims

- State that the company is founder-led.
- Be explicit about what WheelPro Systems builds.
- Use "designed for" and "purpose-built for" language instead of claiming proven results.
- Show realistic frontend product mockups instead of client case studies.
- Avoid named clients, fake logos, fake testimonials, and invented revenue metrics.
- Use honest audit framing: identify where leads are lost and what system could improve the process.

## Current MVP Scope

- Static marketing website using Next.js App Router.
- Responsive pages for Home, Services, Systems, Industries, About, Book, and Contact.
- Reusable component system with Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Lucide React.
- SEO metadata for all public pages.
- Booking URL powered by `NEXT_PUBLIC_BOOKING_URL`.
- Frontend-only system mockups.
