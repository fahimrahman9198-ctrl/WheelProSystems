# WheelPro Systems: per-shop pitch video (`ShopPitch`), 48s

**Who watches:** the owner of one wheel repair shop, on their phone, after we approach them.
**What it must say:** we build (1) a custom, polished website in the latest design, made for their shop, and (2) an admin panel that is a one-stop system for the whole job: their leads, invoices, card payments and lead funnel. The lead funnel gets the most time.
**What it must not say:** "one inbox". No invented results: every number on screen is sample data and is labelled.
**Personalised per shop:** shop name, city, province tax, their demo screenshots and their demo link. `{shop}`, `{city}` and `{tax}` below are filled in by the render script.
**Sound:** 120 BPM music bed (one bar = 2s). Captions carry the message so it works muted. The voiceover line under each scene is optional and the same words as the captions.
