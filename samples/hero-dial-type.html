Curved ticker: 4 typography styles

No icons: a plain needle, and the step, job detail and r/min wrap around the dial. Each section pins while you scroll; scroll down and the needle climbs Lead → Paid, scroll up and it drops back. Only the typography changes between them.

TY1Condensed dash
Condensed dash. Step name in the heading font (Saira Semi Condensed), detail in small spaced capitals, r/min big in blue. Matches the site's headings; reads like a modern digital cluster.
TY2Technical mono
Technical mono. One line in a monospace (JetBrains Mono), numbered like a readout: "03 / FOLLOW-UP EMAILED — DAY 2 · AUTOMATIC — 4,850 RPM". Engineering, data-logger feel.
TY3Wide luxury
Wide luxury. Light, very widely spaced capitals, the step a touch heavier than the detail. Quiet and expensive, like the lettering on a luxury car's dash.
TY4Stacked
Stacked, two lines. The step name large on the outer curve, the detail and r/min small on a second curve just inside it. Strongest hierarchy; the step is readable from across the room.
