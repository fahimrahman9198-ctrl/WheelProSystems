# Shop demo: the shop's website, quote page and admin (/demo/[shop])

Scope: the per-shop demo shown to a wheel shop owner when WheelPro approaches them. The front page is the shop's own customer-facing website (Persuade, for their customers); /quote is the customer photo quote; /admin is Operate. All three share one theme. Not a WheelPro sales page.

Audience: drivers in the shop's city with curb rash, scratches or a bent wheel; judged by the owner, mostly on a phone. Action: get a price from a photo. Constraints: no Western Wheelcraft photos or branding; no invented reviews, prices, timings or results; sample data labelled; noindex.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop's site as an instrument panel for one job: a photo quote you can start in the hero, with a dark readout writing your request back as you pick. Refuses the template shop site of a photo hero, star badges and a services grid.

OWN-WORLD: Warm cream ground, near-black ink, one signal orange, teal only on second lines of headings. General Sans light for big headlines, Instrument Serif italic for one word per heading, Fragment Mono for tracked uppercase labels and readouts (the faces of wheelprosystems.com). Floating rounded nav card with an orange-to-teal scroll line, soft warm shadows, 13px controls, 22px cards, pill chips.

STORY: "Wait, my wheel can be fixed?" They read a hook headline, pick their damage in the panel, see their request written back, add photos, and get a written price. Services and how-it-works answer doubts; the close repeats the action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: city chip, four-word hook headline in light General Sans with one italic serif word over an orange smear, one sentence, dark "Get a price" and an outlined phone button. Right: the photo-quote panel (dark readout, wheels/size/finish tiles, damage chips, orange "Add photos").

FORM: Pinned by the user (collectui "Nexus" instrument-panel landing page reference), adapted to the shop's customers; concept-seed not run. Seed key: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
