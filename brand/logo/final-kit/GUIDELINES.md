# WheelPro Systems — Logo Guidelines (final)

## 1. The logo
- **Idea**: the corner of a car. A fender arches over a wheel, with the tight, even gap that wheel people judge a build by.
- **Versions**: horizontal, one line (primary) · stacked · symbol only · wordmark only.
- **Files**: `svg/` masters and colour variants · `png/` exports · `web/` favicon, app icons, `site.webmanifest`, `head-snippet.html`.
- **Small cut**: `svg/wheelpro-symbol-small.svg` has a wider gap and a bigger hub. Use it below 32 px (the favicon set already does).
- **Construction**: three arcs from one centre (wheel r66, arch r78, hub r20), a front corner of r64, and a 12-unit gap on a 256 grid.

## 2. Clear space
Keep **half the symbol's height** clear on every side. The zone scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 140 px wide | 35 mm wide |
| Stacked | 72 px wide | 20 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px (small cut) | 6 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0A0A0B` | 10 10 11 | Primary: logo, dark grounds |
| Torque Blue | `#2F5BFF` | 47 91 255 | Accent: app tile, highlights, under 5% of a layout |
| Paper | `#F5F5F6` | 245 245 246 | Light grounds, cards |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | Reversed logo |

CMYK and Pantone matches are not set yet; get them from your printer before the first print run.

**Approved pairs**: ink on white or paper · white on ink · white on Torque Blue · Torque Blue on white.

## 5. Typography
- Wordmark: **Mona Sans**. "WheelPro" in **Bold**, "Systems" in **Light**, −2.5% tracking, one line. Stacked version: the same weights on two centred lines. In the files it's converted to outlines.
- Lockup ratio: symbol box height = 1.78 × the wordmark font size; gap between symbol and wordmark = 0.3 × the font size.
- Mona Sans is open source (SIL OFL), so it's safe to use in a logo.
- Website type stays Switzer (UI) and Geist Mono (numbers), as in `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.

## 6. Don'ts
Don't flip the mark (the car faces right, the direction of reading) · don't close or widen the gap · don't add spokes, treads or motion lines · don't stretch, rotate or recolour outside the palette · don't add shadows or gradients · don't retype the wordmark or swap the weights, use the files.
