# WheelPro Systems — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea**: a wheel sliced into four rows that slide sideways, with a hub bore through the middle. It reads as a wheel in motion, and the rows echo the list of booked jobs WheelPro runs for the shop.
- **Versions**: horizontal (primary) · stacked · symbol only · wordmark only.
- **Files**: `svg/` masters and colour variants · `png/` exports · `web/` favicon, app icons, `site.webmanifest`, `head-snippet.html`.
- **Small cut**: `svg/wheelpro-symbol-small.svg` has wider gaps and a bigger hub. Use it below 32 px (the favicon set already does).

## 2. Clear space
Keep **¼ of the symbol's height** clear on every side. The zone scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px (small cut) | 6 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0A0A0B` | 10 10 11 | Primary: logo, dark grounds |
| Torque Blue | `#2F5BFF` | 47 91 255 | Accent: app tile, highlights, under 5% of a layout |
| Paper | `#F5F5F6` | 245 245 246 | Light grounds, cards |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | Reversed logo |

CMYK and Pantone matches are not set yet; get them from your printer's swatch book before the first print run.

**Approved pairs**: ink on white or paper · white on ink · white on Torque Blue · Torque Blue on white.

## 5. Typography
- Wordmark: Inter Semi Bold, −4% tracking, 90% line height, set in two lines aligned right. In the files it's converted to outlines.
- Website type stays Switzer (UI) and Geist Mono (numbers), as in `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.
- Inter is open source (SIL OFL), so it's safe to use in a logo.

## 6. Don'ts
Don't stretch, rotate or recolour outside the palette · don't add shadows, outlines or gradients · don't change the spacing between symbol and wordmark · don't retype the wordmark, use the files · don't put it on busy photos or stripes without the ink tile.

## 7. Open items
- Run a trademark search before launch: striped circles exist in other categories.
- Keep the rows' sideways offsets exactly as drawn (+12, +4, −4, −12); evening them out turns it back into a static striped disc.
